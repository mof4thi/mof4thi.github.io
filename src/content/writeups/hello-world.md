I just finished my MSc in Cybersecurity and AI, and I'm preparing for the HTB CPTS. Both of those
generate a lot of notes, and notes that stay in a private folder tend to rot.

## What to expect

- **Writeups** of retired lab machines and exercises, with the reasoning and not just the commands.
- **Defensive notes** from my time in a SOC, anonymized and with nothing confidential.
- **Projects** I'm building, including what went wrong.

## Ground rules

1. Only retired or practice targets. No active-machine spoilers, no real-world victims.
2. Nothing that identifies an employer or customer.
3. If I got something wrong, I'll fix the post and say so.

If you spot a mistake, the contact page has the best way to reach me.
